# Achievements

Achievements convert wallet behavior into readable reputation moments.

## Initial Categories

- Validator: uptime, consistency, duration, subnet coverage
- Miner: long-term mining activity and subnet participation
- Governance: voting and proposal participation
- Community: verified ecosystem support and educational contributions
- Subnet: early participation and multi-subnet history

## Design Rules

- Achievements should be based on verifiable signals when possible.
- Criteria should be documented and stable.
- Badges should not over-reward easily farmed actions.
- Historical achievements should preserve when score formulas change.
